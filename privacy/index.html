Privacy notice

Updated 2 November 2026

This is Sahand Sharifzadeh’s personal research website. This notice explains its use of Google Analytics and your choices.

Analytics is your choice

Google Analytics 4 is off unless you select Accept. Before you accept, this site does not load the Google Analytics script or send analytics requests to Google. Rejecting does not affect your access to the site.

If you accept, Google Analytics receives information about page views and interactions, approximate location, referring websites, browser and device type, and engagement. Analytics cookies recognise a browser across visits. This helps me understand which research writing people read and improve the site. I do not deliberately send your name or email address to Analytics, and advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled in this site’s tag.

The tag is configured to omit query strings and URL fragments from the page and referrer addresses sent on page views. Google processes connection information such as your IP address to deliver the service and derive approximate location; GA4 says it does not log or store individual IP addresses. This is not a promise that the service is anonymous.

Storage and changing your choice

Your Accept or Reject choice is saved in this browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. The tag sets Analytics cookie lifetimes to 180 days; cookies may be renewed during visits. The preference is specific to this browser and device. If browser storage is blocked, the choice may not survive navigation.

Use Privacy settings below to change your choice at any time. Rejecting after accepting stops further Analytics collection through this tag and removes accessible Analytics cookies for this site. It does not automatically erase data already sent to Google. This privacy page itself does not load Analytics, even after acceptance.

Google’s processing

Google provides the Analytics service and may process data on servers outside the UK. Google’s applicable terms and data-transfer safeguards govern that processing. For more information, read how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Other site services and your rights

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request information independently of the Analytics choice. Your light/dark theme preference is also saved locally. External links and externally hosted images, such as video thumbnails, involve their respective providers when accessed; rejecting Analytics does not block those services.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, erasure, restriction and objection concerning personal data, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority such as the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. For privacy questions, contact me via LinkedIn.
